Gradients for TikZ Studio

Twenty-four gradient families in five styles, each applied to a mock of your editor (New project and Recompile buttons, the active file, the selected variant tab, a softly blended background) and to the login panel. Text stays readable at every point along each gradient.

Why the blending method matters

The same two colours, mixed two ways. Ordinary mixing passes through a dull grey middle; perceptual (OKLCH) mixing stays vivid all the way. Every gradient below uses the perceptual method.
